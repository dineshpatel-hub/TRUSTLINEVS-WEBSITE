1. Scope of Corporate Mandates

Trustline Verification Services executes risk evaluation and evidentiary inquiries exclusively on behalf of registered corporate entities, insurers, third-party administrators (TPAs), financial institutions, and recognized legal practitioners. Trustline does not undertake personal domestic, marital, or unlawful covert surveillance mandates.

2. Evidential Integrity & Strict Legality

All field operatives and analytical staff operate strictly within the bounds of Indian statutory law. Our protocols strictly prohibit illegal wiretapping, entrapment, unauthorized database hacking, or non-consensual biometric extraction. Reports provide factual, verifiable, and legally documented evidence.

3. Turnaround Time (SLA) & Operational Delays

While standard verification turnaround times (TAT) range from 2 to 7 business days, mandates involving remote educational boards, uncooperative prior employers, judicial record backlogs, or extreme monsoon transit delays across rural Gujarat may require extended timelines with real-time client status updates.

4. Privileged Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

All briefings, case materials, witness statements, and final investigation dossiers are protected by strict reciprocal non-disclosure obligations. Findings are delivered exclusively through secure encrypted corporate channels to designated corporate officers.

5. Limitation of Liability

Trustline reports are provided as factual risk assessments to assist corporate decision-makers. While highest professional diligence is applied, final hiring, claim settlement, or litigation decisions rest solely with the commissioning corporate principal.

6. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms and any resulting service contracts are governed by the laws of India. Any legal dispute or proceeding arising hereunder shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
